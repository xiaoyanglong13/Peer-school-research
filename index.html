SCM and OM

MSU offers one undergraduate SCM major; operations management is built into it.

The official award is a B.S. in Supply Chain Management. The program integrates manufacturing operations, purchasing, transportation and physical distribution. There is no separate undergraduate OM major in the official degree list.

How the current estimate is constructed

The registrar reports B.A. and B.S. students separately during MSU's degree transition. Combining those rows gives current, same-term counts for every named major: Finance 1,155; SCM 858; Marketing 517; Accounting 492; Hospitality 377; Management 188; and Human Resources 37.

Denominator matters: SCM is 23.7% of students in named majors, but 20.2% of all Broad undergraduates after including 595 Business-Admitted and 23 Business-Preference students.

For historical context, the department reported 842 SCM undergraduates in Fall 2023. The Spring 2026 registrar count is 16 higher (+1.9%). The older Fall 2022 distribution placed SCM narrowly first; it should now be treated as a historical benchmark rather than the current ranking.

SCM and OM

Scheller awards one B.S. in Business Administration; Operations and Supply Chain Management is an 18-credit concentration, not a standalone major.

Every BSBA student completes one concentration, and Scheller officially recognizes only one concentration on the student's record.

Curriculum and flexibility

Foundation and 18-credit concentration

MGT 3501 Operations Management is in the BSBA core. After it, OSCM students complete six advanced three-credit courses.

Choose four from Group A

MGT 3744 Product, Service and Technology Development

MGT 4401 Supply Chain Modeling

How to read the figures

  • Employment is published only for the BSBA class overall.
  • Concentration salary sample sizes are not shown.
  • The overall mean can be weighted toward larger or higher-paid concentrations.
  • A separate career page says 90% and gives a different signing-bonus mean.
