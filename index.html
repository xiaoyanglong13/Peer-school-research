Interpretation

Best estimate: roughly 27% SCM / 73% other business majors. ?This is based on declared-major counts. Total Broad undergraduate enrollment is a different denominator because it can include students who have not yet declared a major.

Double/additional majors are possible at MSU. ?This means some departmental counts may not map perfectly to unique students. For the purpose of estimating SCM's share of Broad majors, this is treated as a secondary limitation.

A 2025 Broad career report provides a directional cross-check: SCM accounts for 330 graduates in the majors covered by that report.
