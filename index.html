SCM ↔ OM

SCM major; no separate OM major.

Operations is required through SCM 372 Manufacturing and Service Operations Management and integrated with procurement, logistics and analytics.

19-credit major field

SCM 371 Procurement · 372 Operations · 373 Logistics · 475 Analytics · 470 capstone or 472 industry problems · four approved elective credits.

Experience & flexibility

Internship is strongly encouraged, not required. Industry-problem work is optional via SCM 472. Broad360, SCMA, co-op, study abroad, research and case competitions add experience.

Contracting · 1 crTariffs/customs · 1Simulation · 1ERP · 1–3Lean · 1Negotiation · 2Project mgmt · 2

Several are offered on demand; catalog presence does not guarantee annual delivery.

Careers & services

Advertised roles span analyst/consulting, procurement, purchasing, manufacturing supervision, logistics, warehousing and transportation. Infrastructure includes the SCM Council, SCMA employer presentations and career fair, Palmer Career Management, coaching, mock interviews, Handshake, Broad Connect, and the required first-year Wisdom Project alumni interview.

Student themes

Alexa Ryan cited problem solving, optimization, internships and education abroad. Peter Estefan credited practical classes, faculty industry experience and alumni access; an alumni connection led to a Nike internship.

SCM ↔ OM

SCM major; OM embedded.

No separate undergraduate OM major is listed. Required work spans analytics, logistics operations, manufacturing/service operations, sourcing and global strategy.

Required courses

SCM 309 Analytics and Decision Making
SCM 413 / 414 Logistics Operations; Manufacturing & Service Operations
SCM 421 / 460 Strategic Sourcing; Global SCM Strategy
SCM 489 Required internship, 0 credits

Required experience

150+ relevant hours within fewer than six months plus documentation and a reflection video. Internships, co-ops, relevant employment, micro-internships and international internships can qualify.

Flexibility

Business Analytics and Information Management concentrations, collateral option, Heath Integrated Business and Engineering, international-business combinations, Career Ready Certification and Scholars of Distinction. The model favors formal pathways rather than many tiny topic courses.

Career paths & services

Roles include area manager, logistics/procurement specialist, transportation analyst, project manager, consultant and rotational leadership programs. Students use SCM coaches, Walk-in Wednesday, Handshake, fall/spring fairs, speed networking and the Supply Chain Forum. More than 150 companies recruit annually.

Student themes & caveat

Lindsey Cauble combined SCM and accounting, pursued an internship to explore the field, and hoped to apply both to sports operations. Haslam's internship research says students valued meaningful assignments, skill growth, trust and durable professional connections.

Individual profile plus program research; not representative survey evidence.

SCM ↔ OM

B.S. SCM + B.A. Applied Supply Chains; OM embedded.

No separate undergraduate OM major. The STEM B.S. runs at Tempe, Online and ASU Local; the applied B.A. runs at West Valley, Online and ASU Local.

B.S. major core · 18 credits

SCM 315 Analytics · 345 Logistics · 355 Procurement · 432 Operations Management · 455 Research & Negotiation · 479 Strategy. SCM 300 is in the business core; WPC 480 is the capstone.

Career-navigation micro-sequence

WPC 148 · 0.25 cr First-year career development
WPC 248 or 347 · 0.25 Narrative / transfer career management
WPC 348 · 0.25 Networking foundations
WPC 448 · 0.25 Senior career transition

This is the closest peer analogue to workshop-style credits, but the content is career progression rather than current SCM topics.

Experience, paths & services

No universal internship requirement is published. Options include case projects, internship credit, a six-month co-op, capstone, Business Community Advantage, Executive Consortium, organizations and scholarships. Assigned coaches and employer programming support paths into logistics, purchasing, procurement, analytics, management and consulting.

Student evidence gap

Official undergraduate stories credit career coaches and older students with helping identify internships, but a current SCM-specific undergraduate testimonial answering both “why this major?” and “what helped?” was not found.

The dashboard preserves the gap rather than substituting graduate-program stories.

SCM ↔ OM

Combined SCIS major; OM embedded.

No separate OM major. The curriculum joins source–make–deliver operations with demand fulfillment, analytics and information technology.

Required SCM core

SCM 301 Supply Chain · 404 Demand Fulfillment · 405 Manufacturing & Services · 406 Strategic Procurement · 421 Analytics · 450W Strategic Design & Management. MIS 250 supports the IS side.

Experience & flexibility

Internship is not required. Cases, visiting leaders, company research, study abroad and industry partnerships are embedded/available. Students retain three related credits, six supporting credits and 14 open electives. Professional workshops exist, but not a published MSU-like low-credit SCM topic menu.

The Center for Supply Chain Research works with 35+ companies and runs fall/spring fairs; the bulletin reports 6,000+ student/recruiter interactions across five fair days.

Career paths & outcomes detail

Roles include materials scheduler/analyst, production/logistics analyst, procurement, demand planning and consulting. In 2023–24, 88% reported at least one internship; the average signing bonus was $6,070 and 57% received one.

Student themes

Kunjal Kapadia secured an Estée Lauder procurement internship through the SCIS fair. Connor MacEachern said he chose the major partly because supply chain was unfamiliar and he wanted to learn; he highlighted Smeal's support system.

Official individual stories; not representative survey evidence.

SCM ↔ OM

One BSBA; OSCM is an 18-credit concentration.

Every BSBA student completes one concentration and only one is officially recognized. After core MGT 3501 Operations Management, students complete six advanced OSCM courses.

Course menu

Four courses from technology management; product/service development; resource planning; operations strategy; global operations/SCM; service operations; and supply chain modeling, plus two from a broader approved group.

Experience & add-ons

Internship is available, not required; up to three credits may count. The plan can fit two internship semesters and a term abroad. Co-op, research, independent study, global programs, the Supply Chain and Logistics Organization and Tech Square employer access add options.

MGT 3599 Career Development Workshop · 1 credit is required in the BSBA. Certificates may add depth, with restrictions on duplicate areas/double counting.

Career paths & services

Positioning spans operations, logistics, analytics/modeling, technology management, service operations and consulting. Students use career advisors, résumé/interview coaching, employer events, fairs and alumni programming.

OSCM salary is $11,456 (13.9%) below the $82,722 school mean. Concentration-specific employment and salary sample size are not published.

Student theme & caveat

Inika Jain emphasized the intersection of business and technology and the value of a technology-enabled approach to operations.

Official individual profile; not survey evidence.

A separate career page says 90% and gives a different signing-bonus mean; this dashboard uses the dated Class of 2025 report (93%).

Evidence notes

Methods, confidence and comparability

Designed for committee use: every estimate is visibly separated from a published fact, and every outcome is tied to its denominator when available.

Outcome limitations

  • Placement, employment and positive destination are not synonyms.
  • Time windows and knowledge rates differ.
  • ASU has no current undergraduate SCM table.
  • Georgia Tech has concentration salary but no concentration placement or sample size.
  • Testimonials are anecdotes, not representative survey results.
