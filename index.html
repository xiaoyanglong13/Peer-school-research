Why Fisher is adding it

Ohio State’s official proposal says Fisher currently has two highly ranked related specializations—Logistics Management and Operations Management—but no dedicated SCM specialization. The new option is intended to provide clearer branding and a broader, end-to-end supply-chain pathway.

Proposed 21-credit design

The curriculum deliberately combines the three pieces now split across departments:

  • Operations Planning and Control
  • One purchasing course: Purchasing and Supply Management, Purchasing Strategy or Global Sourcing
  • Advanced Logistics Management and Supply Chain Management
  • SCM decision tools and supply-chain technology
  • Logistics and operations/purchasing electives
  • A jointly taught experiential SCM capstone

This design is the clearest evidence that Fisher does not regard either present specialization as already covering integrated SCM.

Curriculum and flexibility

Required management foundation

Probability/statistics, communication, managerial psychology, microeconomics and accounting, plus restricted electives across finance, operations, marketing and strategy.

MIT degree chart ↗

Five-subject concentration

Students take five full subjects, at least three in Course 15; Operations Management includes 15.7611 Introduction to Operations Management. Students may use a defined or faculty-approved individualized concentration, leaving 48–96 unrestricted units.

Different meaning of “small course”: MIT counts units rather than semester credits; half-subjects are typically six units. This is flexible modularity, but not the same construct as MSU’s one-credit topical workshops.

Experiential learning

Action Learning

MIT Sloan’s project-based ecosystem connects teams with organizations and live management problems.

Undergraduate research

UROP allows paid, for-credit or volunteer faculty research across MIT.

Entrepreneurship and cross-registration

Institute-wide electives, labs and venture resources let students connect operations to engineering and technology.

Job services and networking

MIT Career Advising & Professional Development offers advising, recruiting, career fairs, Handshake, interview preparation and employer/alumni access. Sloan’s undergraduate team supplies academic and career guidance.

Concentrations and add-ons

Students select 9–12 guided-elective credits in concentrations such as Digital Supply Chain. Fast Track permits qualified seniors to apply selected graduate courses toward both bachelor’s and master’s requirements. A 127-credit Marketing + SCMA double major is available.
