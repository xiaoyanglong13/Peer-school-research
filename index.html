SCM and OM

MSU offers one undergraduate SCM major; operations management is built into it.

The official award is a B.S. in Supply Chain Management. The program integrates manufacturing operations, purchasing, transportation and physical distribution. There is no separate undergraduate OM major in the official degree list.

Best interpretation

The best apples-to-apples estimate is approximately 27% SCM / 73% other declared business majors. The 2023 department count signals substantial growth, but it should not replace the 2022 share without matched counts.

Confidence: high for the 2022 comparison; medium for interpreting the 2023 increase. Double/additional majors and changing departmental counting can prevent headcounts from mapping perfectly to unique students.

Curriculum and flexibility

19-credit major field

SCM 371 · Procurement and Supply Management · 3 cr
Purchasing process, supplier relationships, negotiation, commodity planning and cost/value analysis.
SCM 372 · Manufacturing and Service Operations · 3 cr
Planning, scheduling, capacity, shop-floor control and just-in-time systems.
SCM 373 · Logistics and Transportation · 3 cr
Distribution, facilities, carriers, transportation costing and customer service.
SCM 475 · Data Analytics in SCM · 3 cr
Descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics, modeling and programming.
SCM 470 or SCM 472 · 3 cr
Integrated case capstone, or industry problems completed with Engineering.
Department-approved selections · 4 cr
Creates room for low-credit specialties and other approved courses.

Experiential learning

Inside the degree

SCM 472 applies integrated SCM problem solving to industry problems in collaboration with Engineering. It is available as an alternative to SCM 470, so it is not required of every major.

Course details ↗

Broad requirement

All Broad B.S. students complete an Integrative Experiences Requirement addressing cultural/community competency, entrepreneurial, ethical, global and philanthropic mindsets, and strategic leadership.

Beyond the classroom

The department encourages internships, co-ops, study abroad and honors. Broad360 and SCMA add case competitions, research, employer interaction and other engagement.

Broad360 ↗
Internship status: strongly recommended, not required for graduation. Broad permits up to three internship credits toward the B.S.

How to read the figures

  • Outcomes were collected within six months through a First Destination Survey plus LinkedIn, following NACE standards.
  • The overall MSU Class of 2025 knowledge rate is 73%; a separate SCM-specific knowledge rate is not published.
  • Salaries are self-reported by U.S.-based, full-time-employed graduates and exclude bonuses.
  • “Employment rate” and “positive destination rate” are different measures, especially for majors with many graduates continuing education.
