How the current estimate is constructed

The registrar reports B.A. and B.S. students separately during MSU's degree transition. Combining those rows gives current, same-term counts for every named major: Finance 1,155; SCM 858; Marketing 517; Accounting 492; Hospitality 377; Management 188; and Human Resources 37.

Denominator matters: SCM is 23.7% of students in named majors, but 20.2% of all Broad undergraduates after including 595 Business-Admitted and 23 Business-Preference students.

For historical context, the department reported 842 SCM undergraduates in Fall 2023. The Spring 2026 registrar count is 16 higher (+1.9%). The older Fall 2022 distribution placed SCM narrowly first; it should now be treated as a historical benchmark rather than the current ranking.

Five-subject concentration

Students take five full subjects, at least three in Course 15; Operations Management includes 15.7611 Introduction to Operations Management. Students may use a defined or faculty-approved individualized concentration, leaving 48–96 unrestricted units.
